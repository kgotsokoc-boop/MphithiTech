Biography

The person and the mission behind Mphithi-Tech Holdings.

Mr Comfort Oratile Kgotsoko is the founder of Mphithi-Tech Holdings (Pty) Ltd and a software developer currently studying at Tshwane University of Technology (TUT). His passion lies in solving the technical problems that most people overlook — application failures, system faults, and the hidden issues that cause websites to underperform.

Driven by a belief that reliable technology should be accessible to every business, Comfort founded Mphithi-Tech Holdings to bridge the gap between complex technical challenges and practical, affordable solutions. His academic background, combined with hands-on troubleshooting experience, allows him to approach problems with both theoretical depth and real-world pragmatism.

Under his leadership, Mphithi-Tech Holdings is committed to building a reputation for excellence, integrity, and innovation — positioning itself as a trusted technology partner for businesses across South Africa and beyond.
